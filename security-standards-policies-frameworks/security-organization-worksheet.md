# Security Organization Worksheet

## Part 1: Describe Your Security Interests

- Raising security awareness
- Protecting sensitive data
- Forensics

## Part 2: Identify Three Organizations and Their Mission or Objectives

**Organization 1: SANS Institute** — a cybersecurity training and research organization whose mission is to provide practical, hands-on security training, certifications, and awareness resources that help organizations and individuals defend against cyber threats.

**Organization 2: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)** — a U.S. federal agency whose mission includes developing cybersecurity standards, guidelines, and frameworks (such as the NIST CSF and SP 800-53) that organizations use to protect sensitive data and manage risk.

**Organization 3: High Technology Crime Investigation Association (HTCIA)** — a professional association whose mission is to provide education, resources, and collaboration for digital forensics investigators and law enforcement working on high-tech and cyber-enabled crime.

## Part 3: Write Out Which Organization Aligns with Your Interests and Why

**Organization 1 (SANS Institute):** their focus on security training and awareness programs directly aligns with my interest in raising security awareness — a lot of real-world breaches (like weak or shared passwords) come down to untrained employees, which SANS's training model is built to address.

**Organization 2 (NIST):** their standards, like SP 800-53, connect to my interest in protecting sensitive data by giving concrete, actionable guidelines instead of vague best practices, which is useful for actually implementing controls rather than just talking about them.

**Organization 3 (HTCIA):** this is the closest match to my actual career direction, since it's built specifically around digital forensics investigation, which is my main area of interest.

**Best choice for my professional interests:** HTCIA — because my primary goal is to work in digital forensics and incident response, and this organization is built specifically around that field, rather than security in general.
